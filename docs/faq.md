### Why is Awxynth so barebones?
Awxynth uses maths. As it turns out, a lot of the effects conventional synthesisers provide, like distortion and compression, are easily achieved through raw maths. It’s just graph transformations and function composition :0

I’ve provided some common effects to get you started, but really, the whole point of Awxynth is to let you explore using maths to shape your sounds!
